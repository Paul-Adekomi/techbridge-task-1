What TechBridge is

TechBridge is an internship and learning initiative run by Baselink Services Limited. It helps learners take what they know and use it on practical work.

What we offer

A 30-day practical internship with programs in Data Analytics and Web Development.

Who it is for

Learners and aspiring interns who want hands-on experience beyond tutorials and classrooms.

Why join

You work on project-based tasks and can earn a recommendation letter based on your successful participation and performance.

Current programs

Two programs are open right now. Pick the one that fits the path you want to grow in.

Work with data to find patterns, answer questions and present your findings clearly through practical, project-based tasks.

Web Development
